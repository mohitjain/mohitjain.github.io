I'm Mohit Jain, a Principal Engineer with about sixteen years of experience building software. Most of it has gone into distributed systems, enterprise data platforms and software architecture, and into the quieter work that makes large systems dependable: reliability, operations and careful technical decisions.

Right now much of my learning time goes into artificial intelligence: how foundation models work, how to give them the right context, how agents are built and run, and how to tell whether any of it is reliable. I'm studying the field through books, papers and lectures, and by building things myself.

I also read well beyond technology: engineering leadership, organizational design, strategy, decision-making and systems thinking. I've come to see these as one subject with the technical work, which is how complex things are understood, designed and led.

This site is where I write it down. Some pieces are short notes on something I learned, some are longer explanations, and some are accounts of things I built, including what went wrong. It is a record of how my thinking changes over time, so it will wander as my interests do.

## What to expect

- Plain, careful writing. I'll try to separate what is established, what I read in a source, what I interpret, and what I'm only guessing.
- Not every article is original research, and not every article is long.
- No publishing schedule.

## How I write

I use AI tools to help organize notes and improve clarity. The understanding, the judgment and the mistakes are mine. Opinions here are my own.

Find the code and experiments on [GitHub](https://github.com/mohitjain), or [follow new writing](/subscribe/).
