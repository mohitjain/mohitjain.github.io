# The Craft of Engineering

*Systems, Intelligence, Architecture & Ideas.*

A personal knowledge journal about engineering, technology, and the ideas that shape how we think
and build. Written by Mohit Jain.

**Read it at [mohitjain.github.io](https://mohitjain.github.io).**

## What you will find

- **Architecture:** the principles behind designing and operating complex software systems.
- **AI:** the foundations and engineering of modern AI systems.
- **Leadership & Thinking:** how engineers lead, decide and think: the human side of building
  software.
- **Reading:** a record of the articles, books, papers and videos I have read, with the few I would
  recommend most. Nothing is listed until I have actually read it.

The idea behind all of it: learn deeply, think clearly, build thoughtfully.
