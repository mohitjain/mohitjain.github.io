## The question

Can I build, from first principles and in the open, a system that ingests the material I read and answers questions over it with citations? That means document ingestion and chunking, hybrid retrieval, a small agent harness, memory, and an evaluation set that tells me whether any of it works.

## Why

Reading explains how these pieces are meant to work. Building them shows where the explanations and the implementation differ, and measuring them shows what actually matters.
