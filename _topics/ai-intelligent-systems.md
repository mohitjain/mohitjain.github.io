## What it covers

- Foundation models and LLMs
- Transformers and model fundamentals
- Context engineering, retrieval and RAG
- Agent architecture, runtimes, memory and tools
- Evaluation, reliability and reward hacking
- Model adaptation, inference economics, security and governance
- Enterprise AI systems

## Why it matters

AI is changing what software is made of. Understanding how these systems work, and how they fail, matters more than keeping up with each new release.

## What belongs here

Concept explanations, reading notes on papers and books, and engineering views of agents, retrieval and evaluation.
