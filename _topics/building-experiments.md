## What it covers

- Building systems from scratch
- Technical experiments and prototypes
- Embeddings and retrieval experiments
- Model training and serving
- AI-assisted software development
- Measurements, failures and lessons

## Why it matters

You understand a system differently once you have built a small version of it. The measurements and the mistakes are the useful part.

## What belongs here

Build logs and experiment write-ups: what I wanted to build, how I approached it, what the measurements showed, and what I would change.
