## What it covers

- Technical leadership and influence without authority
- Architecture ownership and technical decision-making
- Organizational design and team topology
- Engineering strategy, execution and prioritization
- Communication and writing

## Why it matters

Systems are built by teams, and the structure of the team and the clarity of the strategy shape the result as much as the code does.

## What belongs here

Ideas from books and experience about leading technical work, making decisions, and communicating them well.
